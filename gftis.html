🏫 About GFTIs

Government Funded Technical Institutes (GFTIs) are a premier group of government-supported technical institutions offering undergraduate engineering, architecture, and technology programs. Most GFTIs participate directly in the annual JoSAA and CSAB counselling processes based on JEE Main Ranks.

If you are preparing for JEE, explore the JEE syllabus, read JEE preparation tips, and compare IITs, NITs, and IIITs.

📅 Development Timeline

1960s – 1980s

Several central and state-funded engineering colleges were established across India to promote industrial technical knowledge.

2000s – JoSAA Integration

Many prominent technical institutes unified under central seat allocation systems (AIEEE, later JoSAA/CSAB).

Today

Over 40 GFTIs provide quality engineering, architecture, and applied research education with specialized infrastructure.
