🏫 About GFTIs

Government Funded Technical Institutes (GFTIs) are engineering institutions financially supported by the Government of India. They offer quality technical education at affordable fees and admit students mainly through JEE Main and JoSAA counselling.

📅 Development Timeline

1960s

Several government engineering colleges were established across India.

2000+

Many institutes became part of the JoSAA admission system.

Today

GFTIs continue to provide quality engineering education in multiple disciplines.
