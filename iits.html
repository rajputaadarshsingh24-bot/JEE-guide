🏛 About IITs

The Indian Institutes of Technology (IITs) are India's most prestigious engineering institutions. Renowned for academic excellence, cutting-edge research, innovation, and entrepreneurship, IITs have consistently produced world-class engineers, scientists, entrepreneurs, and leaders. Admission to undergraduate programs is through JEE Advanced after qualifying JEE Main.
