🏛 About Indian Institutes of Technology (IITs)

The Indian Institutes of Technology (IITs) are India's most prestigious public technical institutions, world-renowned for excellence in engineering education, cutting-edge scientific research, and global technological innovation.

Undergraduate admissions into B.Tech and Dual Degree programs require candidates to first clear JEE Main, qualify for JEE Advanced, and secure a high All India Rank (AIR) followed by centralized seat allocation via JoSAA counselling.

Before applying, make sure to review the official JEE syllabus and strategic JEE preparation tips. You can also compare IITs with other premier colleges like NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs.
