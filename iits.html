🏛 About IITs

The Indian Institutes of Technology (IITs) are a group of premier public technical institutions in India known for engineering education, scientific research, innovation and entrepreneurship. For undergraduate engineering admission, students qualify through JEE Main, appear for JEE Advanced, and then participate in the JoSAA counselling process for seat allocation.

Before exploring IITs, you can also check the JEE syllabus and JEE preparation tips. You can also compare IITs with NITs, IIITs and GFTIs.
