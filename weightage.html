💡 JEE GUIDE Smart Strategy Pro-Tips

1. Do Not Skip Low Weightage Chapters

Easy questions from smaller chapters (e.g. Measurement Error, Units & Dimensions) award the exact same 4 marks with significantly less time investment.

2. High Return Anchors

Prioritize 3D Geometry, Vectors, Coordination Compounds, Electrostatics, and Thermodynamics early to secure >45% of total score.

3. Multi-Concept Mastery for Advanced

JEE Advanced links Mechanics with Electrostatics, and GOC with Thermodynamics. Focus on conceptual interconnections.
