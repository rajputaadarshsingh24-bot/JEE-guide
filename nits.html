🏫 About NITs

National Institutes of Technology (NITs) are autonomous public technical institutions in India known for engineering education, research and innovation. Admission to undergraduate engineering programs is primarily based on JEE Main performance, followed by JoSAA counselling and seat allocation.

If you are preparing for JEE, you can also explore the JEE syllabus, JEE preparation tips, and learn about IITs, IIITs and GFTIs.

📅 History Timeline

1959

Regional Engineering Colleges (RECs) were established.

2002

RECs were upgraded and renamed as National Institutes of Technology.

Today

31 NITs provide world-class engineering education across India.
