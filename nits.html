🏫 About NITs

National Institutes of Technology (NITs) are autonomous public technical institutions in India renowned for excellence in engineering education, research, and technological innovation. Admission to undergraduate engineering programs (B.Tech) is based on JEE Main performance, followed by JoSAA counselling and seat allocation.

If you are preparing for JEE, you can also explore the JEE syllabus, JEE preparation tips, and learn about IITs, IIITs, and GFTIs.

📅 History Timeline

1959

Regional Engineering Colleges (RECs) were established across India to foster regional technical education.

2002

RECs were upgraded and officially renamed as National Institutes of Technology (NITs) under central governance.

Today

31 premier NITs provide world-class engineering education and cutting-edge research opportunities.

📍 Quick Facts

🌍 Nationwide Presence

31 NITs are strategically located across various states and union territories of India.

🎯 Entrance Exam

Undergraduate admission is purely based on JEE Main All India Ranks through centralized JoSAA counselling.

🎓 Degrees Offered

Programs offered include B.Tech, M.Tech, M.Sc., MBA, MCA, and Ph.D. research degrees.
