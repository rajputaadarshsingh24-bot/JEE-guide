📖 About JEE

Joint Entrance Examination (JEE) is India's premier engineering entrance examination. It is conducted for admission into NITs, IIITs, and other centrally funded technical institutes through JEE Main, while JEE Advanced is conducted for admission into the IITs.

🎓 Eligibility

  • Passed Class 12 with PCM.
  • Students appearing in Class 12 are also eligible.
  • JEE Main has multiple attempts every year.
  • Top qualifiers of JEE Main can appear for JEE Advanced.
  • Admission depends on rank, eligibility and institute criteria.
