💻 About IIITs

Indian Institutes of Information Technology (IIITs) are a group of premier institutes focused on Information Technology, Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Electronics, and related fields. They are known for industry-oriented education, research, innovation, and excellent placements in the technology sector.

📅 History Timeline

1997

The first IIIT was established in Hyderabad.

2014

Many new IIITs were established under the Public-Private Partnership (PPP) model.

Today

26 IIITs are providing quality education in Information Technology and allied fields.
