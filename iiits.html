💻 About IIITs

Indian Institutes of Information Technology (IIITs) are premier higher education institutions focused on Information Technology, Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Electronics, and allied fields. They are known for technology-focused education, research, innovation, and direct industry collaboration.

Admission to most IIITs is based on JEE Main performance followed by JoSAA or CSAB counselling, while institutes like IIIT Hyderabad conduct independent entrance exams (UGEE) as well.

If you are preparing for JEE, explore the JEE syllabus, read JEE preparation tips, and compare IITs, NITs, and GFTIs.

📅 History Timeline

1997

The first IIIT was established in Hyderabad as an autonomous institute focusing on IT and software research.

2014

The IIIT PPP Act was passed, establishing several new IIITs under Public-Private Partnerships involving central/state governments and industry partners.

Today

26 IIITs operate across the country delivering industry-aligned education in Artificial Intelligence, Software Engineering, and Robotics.

📍 Quick Key Highlights

💼 Excellent Placements

IIITs consistently report top-tier average packages with global technology firms, software engineering labs, and product companies.

🏢 Industry Collaboration

Many IIITs work directly with IT industry partners to design dynamic, updated course curricula aligned with current market demands.

🔬 Research & Innovation

Emphasis is placed on early research, incubator support, coding cultures, and student entrepreneurship right from undergraduate levels.
