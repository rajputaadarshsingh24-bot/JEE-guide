💻 About IIITs

Indian Institutes of Information Technology (IIITs) are institutes focused on Information Technology, Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, Electronics and related fields. They are known for technology-focused education, research, innovation and industry collaboration. Admission to many IIITs is based on JEE Main performance followed by JoSAA or CSAB counselling, although admission routes can vary between individual institutes.

If you are preparing for JEE, explore the JEE syllabus, JEE preparation tips, and compare IITs, NITs and GFTIs.
