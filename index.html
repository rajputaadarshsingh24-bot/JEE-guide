What is JEE (Joint Entrance Examination)?

The Joint Entrance Examination (JEE) is a national-level engineering entrance assessment in India comprising JEE Main and JEE Advanced for admissions into premier engineering colleges like IITs, NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs.
